ABOUT NPT48

「NPT48（New Planet 48）」กลุ่มไอดอลเสมือนจริงสัญชาติไทย

NPT48 (short for New Planet 48) is a nonprofit Thai virtual idol group with sky blue as its official color, inspired by the calm of the mountains and the sea.

Like BNK48, NPT48 holds regular auditions for new members. Successful applicants begin as trainees — a team that stands by to support the official members across all activities.

When an official member graduates from the group, a trainee is promoted to fill the vacancy, allowing NPT48 to carry on with renewed energy.

The group is committed to sincere, approachable performances, sharing positivity, freshness, and natural smiles with audiences everywhere.
